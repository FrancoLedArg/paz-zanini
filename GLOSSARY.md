# Paz Zanini — Manuales

The public archive of Prof. Raúl E. Paz Zanini's free statistics and data-science manuals: a place to discover them, find them again, and take them away.

## Catalog

**Manual**:
One of the Autor's books, published as a single PDF.
_Avoid_: Recurso, libro, documento, PDF (as a name for the concept)

**Autor**:
Raúl E. Paz Zanini, Magíster en Ciencia de Datos — the sole author of every Manual.
_Avoid_: Profesor (in UI copy), Raul (without accent)

**Colección**:
One of three groups every Manual belongs to exactly one of: Estadística desde cero, Ciencia de datos desde cero, Estadística aplicada.
_Avoid_: Serie, categoría, sección

**Recorrido**:
The suggested reading order, in steps, of the Manuales in a "desde cero" Colección; a Par shares one step.
_Avoid_: Ruta, nivel

**Campo**:
A field of study grouping the Manuales of Estadística aplicada: Salud, Tecnología, Ciencias Sociales.
_Avoid_: Área, rama

**Carrera**:
The university degree a Manual of Estadística aplicada is written for (e.g. Obstetricia, Periodismo).
_Avoid_: Disciplina, profesión

**Par**:
Two Manuales that are the same book in R and in Python; each is a full Manual that links to the other.
_Avoid_: Edición, versión

**Herramienta**:
The software a Manual teaches with: R, Python, Excel, or none.
_Avoid_: Lenguaje, tecnología

## Presentation

**Hélice**:
The arrangement of a Colección's page: its Manuales as books floating along a spiral, one book in focus at a time. Each Colección has its own.
_Avoid_: Pila (the stack it replaced), espiral, archivo, estante, galería

**Lomo**:
A book's spine, showing the Manual's title.
_Avoid_: Canto, borde

**Tapa**:
The cover the site designs for a Manual, consistent across all Manuales; distinct from the cover printed inside the PDF.
_Avoid_: Portada, cover

## Use

**Descargar**:
Saving a Manual's PDF to the visitor's device; the primary way a Manual is used.
_Avoid_: Bajar, obtener

**Hojear**:
Previewing a Manual's pages inside the site, before deciding to Descargar it.
_Avoid_: Leer online, ver

**Visita de descubrimiento**:
A first visit, where the visitor explores what Manuales exist.

**Visita de regreso**:
A later visit, where the visitor already knows which Manual they want and needs to reach it fast.
